RoundKaki Terms

Release v2.4 · Updated 6 October 2026

RoundKaki is an early-stage golf scoring and social-game tracker. Golfers are responsible for reviewing handicaps, stroke allocations, course details and house rules before play.

Points, not payment processing

RoundKaki records points/units for golf games. It does not accept stakes, hold money, settle wagers or process payouts. Users are responsible for complying with applicable laws and club rules.

Course and GPS information

GPS distances and public map/course information are approximate and may be incomplete. They should not be relied on for safety-critical navigation or official tournament rulings.

MVP availability

This release is a public testing candidate. Features, scoring rules, course data and data formats may change as RoundKaki develops. Users should verify official course scorecards and competition rules before play.
