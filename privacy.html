RoundKaki Privacy Notice

Release v3.4 · Updated 6 October 2026

RoundKaki can be used as a guest or with a registered account. Guest data remains on the device. Registered users can choose cloud-backed account and game data through Supabase.

Account information

When a user creates an account, RoundKaki collects the user’s name, RoundKaki ID, email address, mobile number, golf handicap and preferred tee. Email is used for authentication and account-recovery emails. Mobile number is stored as private account information. Other signed-in golfers may later be able to see only the user’s RoundKaki ID, display name, handicap and preferred tee for golf-related features.

Golf and game data

For signed-in users with cloud sync enabled, RoundKaki can store player profiles, Kaki-stroke history, active/completed rounds, game results and UP/SINGLE declarations in the user’s private cloud state. Public course-cache data remains device-local and can be downloaded again. Row-level security is configured so that the private account and app-state rows are accessible only to their authenticated owner.

Location

RoundKaki requests location only when the user chooses a GPS feature such as Find Course Near Me or the in-round GPS hole helper. For nearby-course discovery, coordinates may be sent to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service so nearby mapped golf courses can be returned. RoundKaki does not continuously track location in the background.

Course data

RoundKaki maintains a shared course library. Course scorecards may be populated from public/open golf data sources, optional third-party course-data services, club scorecards and golfer submissions. Nearby-course and mapped-hole information may be obtained from OpenStreetMap/Overpass. Submitted scorecards can be stored for review before being marked verified. Coverage and accuracy vary, so golfers should review course details before play.

Payments

RoundKaki tracks game points/units only. It does not hold funds, process wagers, transfer money or provide a cash payout service.

Local controls

Users can export or reset local RoundKaki data from the app. Signing out does not automatically delete local guest data from the device.

Service provider

Registered account authentication and cloud database functions are provided through Supabase. Supabase receives data necessary to provide those services under its own service terms and privacy practices.

Scorecard scanning

When you use Scan Scorecard, the selected photo is processed in your browser for text recognition. RoundKaki does not automatically upload or store the original scorecard image. Structured values such as course layout, hole number, Par, Stroke Index and tee distance are submitted only when you explicitly choose to share a scorecard for verification.
