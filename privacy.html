RoundKaki Privacy Notice

Release v2.4 · Updated 6 October 2026

RoundKaki is currently a local-first golf scoring application. Player profiles, Kaki stroke history, rounds and cached course details are stored on the user’s device using browser/app storage unless the user exports a backup.

Location

RoundKaki requests location only when the user chooses a GPS feature such as Find Course Near Me or the in-round GPS hole helper. For nearby-course discovery, the requested coordinates are sent to the public OpenStreetMap Overpass service so nearby mapped golf courses can be returned. RoundKaki does not continuously track location in the background.

Golf and game data

The current MVP does not require an account and does not upload player names, scores, UP/SINGLE declarations, Kaki strokes or game history to a RoundKaki cloud server. Data remains on the device unless the user chooses to export it.

Payments

RoundKaki tracks game points/units only. It does not hold funds, process wagers, transfer money or provide a cash payout service.

Third-party data

Course scorecard data may be requested from the keyless OpenGolfAPI and cached locally for offline reuse. Nearby course and mapped hole information may be obtained from OpenStreetMap/Overpass. An optional GolfCourseAPI key may be configured only as a fallback; if provided, it is stored locally on the device. Coverage and accuracy vary, so golfers should review course details before play.

Deletion

Users can reset local RoundKaki data from the app’s data/settings area, or remove the app/site data through their browser or device settings.
